Chapter I · My Story

From Dschang
to Berlin

I was born and raised in Dschang, Cameroon. Like many, I grew up with a curiosity for how things work — computers, networks, systems. That curiosity became a passion.

I earned my BSc in IT Engineering from the University of Dschang, then a Master's in Software Engineering & AI. But I knew I wanted more. So I packed my bags and moved to Berlin.

Today, I'm pursuing a Master's in Computer Science at TU Berlin, holding certifications from EC-Council, Cisco, and ANSSI, and building CYBERILL — my initiative to make cybersecurity accessible.
